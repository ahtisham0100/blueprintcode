Our mission

A stronger community starts by putting young people at its centre.

Woodlawn Restorative Justice Hub engages young people and families in building safer, more connected communities. Our work creates space to be heard, tools to resolve conflict and pathways into long-term leadership.

Three parts of one shared future.

Lasting change is more than a single programme. It grows when restoration, opportunity and community move together.
