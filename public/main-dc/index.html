Does the ADA apply to my website?

Courts increasingly treat websites as "places of public accommodation" under Title III. If you sell to or serve the public in the U.S., you are a potential target — regardless of company size.

Aren't accessibility overlays enough?

No. Overlay widgets have been named in hundreds of lawsuits and cannot fix the underlying code. Genuine conformance requires remediating the site itself.

How long does an audit take?

Most small-business audits are delivered in 5–14 business days, depending on the number of unique page templates and interactive flows.

What do I receive at the end?

A prioritized findings report, a developer-ready fix guide with code examples, a VPAT, and — after re-test — a compliance certificate.
