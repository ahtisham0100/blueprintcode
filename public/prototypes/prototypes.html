PROTO-01 — LIFTS & ARCHITECTURAL INTERIORS

Mirrored elevator cabin.

A mirrored cabin and a brushed one look almost identical in a flat product photo. The finish is most of the price and none of the picture, so buyers postpone the decision or default to the safe option.

REAL-TIME 3DDESKTOP + MOBILE

What it proves

That a reflective interior can be shown honestly in a browser, at speed, without a render farm.

  • True planar reflection, computed live rather than faked with a texture
  • Doors that open and close, so the cabin reads as a space
  • Environment-aware lighting — the mirror reflects the configured surroundings
  • Finish switching with no reload and no visible pop
  • Holds frame rate on a mid-range phone

PROTO-02 — JEWELLERY & PERSONALISED GOODS

Charm bracelet builder.

A customisable piece has more combinations than a catalogue can photograph. The customer is asked to imagine the thing they're paying for, which is exactly when they close the tab.

DRAG & DROPSINGLE FILE

What it proves

That a personalised product can be assembled by hand, on a phone, and still load instantly.

  • Drag to rearrange charms along the chain, tap to select
  • Pre-rendered assets, so there is no 3D download to wait for
  • Live price as pieces are added and removed
  • Ships as a single self-contained file — no build step, no framework
  • The finished arrangement is what gets ordered, not an approximation

PROTO-03 — MARINE & POWERED EQUIPMENT

Outboard motor selector.

"What size do I need?" is the question every marine dealer answers by phone, one customer at a time. The honest answer depends on hull, length, weight and how the boat gets used — which is a conversation, not a filter.

DECISION ENGINENO 3D REQUIRED

What it proves

That expert sizing advice can be encoded once and given away at scale, without dumbing it down.

  • Plain-English questions in, technical specification out
  • A long catalogue narrowed to a short shortlist
  • Each recommendation carries the reason it was chosen
  • Handles "I'm not sure" without dead-ending the customer
  • Captures the enquiry with the spec already attached
