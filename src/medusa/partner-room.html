The system behind the Hunt.

Medusa Heads is an in-house SaaS product developed by Blueprint Code AI. The public experience is deliberately simple. Underneath it is a structured system for defining commercial judgement, gathering evidence and turning that evidence into qualified opportunities.

THE COMPOUNDING COMMERCIAL LOOP

The flow is not a straight line. Every measured outcome returns upstream as structured learning. The next Hunt starts from a stronger position, so the loop repeats at a higher level rather than simply going round in a circle.

01 / ARCHITECTURE

Judgement before outreach

A Medusa Head represents what a customer considers worth finding. A Hunt applies an approved version of that judgement to a candidate universe, collecting evidence before an opportunity is passed downstream.

02 / INTEGRATION

A clean boundary

Medusa is designed to research, qualify and explain the opportunity — and, at Enrich level, identify the likely people and contact context around it. The downstream partner remains the execution engine: CRM, outreach, sales automation, advertising, workflow or other action at scale. The boundary keeps each platform focused on what it does best.

03 / BETA

Prove the targeting effect

First we run Medusa as Client Zero and establish our own baseline. If the results justify it, we then work with a partner to identify a small opt-in beta group from suitable users and compare evidence-qualified Medusa opportunities against their normal targeting process.

04 / EVIDENCE

Measure outcomes, not theatre

Primary measures are positive replies, qualified meetings, opportunities and downstream revenue. Opens and superficial engagement are supporting signals, not the definition of success.

05 / LEARNING

Close the loop

Outcomes can feed back into Medusa as structured evidence. The system can recommend changes to a Head, but active judgement is not silently rewritten: proposed learning remains subject to human approval.

06 / COMMERCIAL

Partnership before dependency

The architecture remains provider-neutral. The aim is friendship and proof before commercial structure: prove the baseline ourselves, then test with the partner if the numbers justify it.

Cerberus joins the Hunt

Medusa hunts. Cerberus guards the gate.

Medusa qualifies the company. Enrich identifies the likely people around the opportunity. For higher-value Hunts, Cerberus adds a deeper people-verification pass before release. Cerberus Pack verifies the existing commercial case in greater depth, while Cerberus Gate can be run after delivery on selected Pack-checked opportunities where the permitted-use and jurisdiction context also needs to be checked before handoff.

THE CERBERUS PASS
HEAD FINDS→ HUNT COLLECTS EVIDENCE→ CERBERUS VERIFIES PEOPLE→ PACK VERIFIES COMMERCIAL CASE→ GATE CHECKS PERMITTED USE→ QUALIFIED HANDOFF→ PARTNER EXECUTES
MEDUSA / INCLUDED

Qualify the company

Every delivered Medusa result has been evidence-qualified against the active Head: the organisation is relevant to the Hunt and supported by evidence before it consumes a Coin.

ENRICH / PEOPLE DISCOVERY

Find the likely people

Enrich identifies likely decision-makers, roles, contact details and useful context around the qualified company. It discovers the people; it does not claim to have stress-tested them.

CERBERUS / PEOPLE VERIFICATION

Verify the people

Cerberus verifies role accuracy, whether the person is still at the company, seniority, decision relevance, company relationship, source freshness and material contradictions before the opportunity is released.

CERBERUS PACK / COMMERCIAL VERIFICATION

Verify the commercial case

Cerberus Pack includes the people checks, then verifies the existing opportunity in greater commercial depth using relevant licensed financial, ownership, risk, filing and commercial evidence where available. Pack verifies deeper; it does not broaden the Hunt.

CERBERUS GATE / PERMITTED-USE CHECK

Check permitted use before handoff

Gate is not a standalone research tier. It runs after delivery only on Pack-checked opportunities you select for handoff, checking target jurisdiction, applicable outreach rules, source and licence provenance, suppression or objection status, retention or use restrictions and whether the Pack findings can be passed downstream for the intended purpose.

PURSUE / OUTCOME LOOP

Activate the handoff

Pursue connects the qualified opportunity to the customer's chosen outreach engine, passes the approved opportunity downstream and returns measurable outcomes to Medusa. The execution platform handles outreach; Medusa uses the results to sharpen the next Hunt.

STANDARD HUNT VS CERBERUS HUNT

Medusa qualifies the company. Enrich finds the people. Cerberus verifies the people. Cerberus Pack verifies the commercial case. Cerberus Gate checks whether selected Pack findings can be used for the intended handoff.

Scale without rebuilding

One architecture. Many markets.

Medusa is being designed so international expansion is a configuration problem rather than a rebuild. A Head defines what matters; the market layer determines where Medusa looks, which sources it trusts, which languages it works in and which providers are used to complete the Hunt.

MARKET DEPLOYMENT

Country by country — without starting again

Where suitable data sources and providers exist, a new market can be prepared by configuring geography, language, source intelligence, evidence rules and local operating requirements around the same underlying Medusa architecture.

PARTNER SCALE

Follow the partner's footprint

A successful Medusa × execution-partner model does not need to remain tied to one launch territory. The same integration pattern can be deployed into additional countries and customer markets as each market is validated.

LOCAL INTELLIGENCE

Local sources. Common judgement model.

Different countries may require different directories, websites, languages, enrichment services and evidence providers. Those differences belong in the source and provider layers — not in a separate product build.

COMMERCIAL POTENTIAL

The opportunity compounds.

Once the core partnership loop is proven, each additional supported market expands the addressable opportunity without requiring Medusa and the execution partner to reinvent the relationship each time.

THE SCALE MODEL
PROVE ONE MARKET→ LOCALISE SOURCES + LANGUAGE→ REUSE THE INTEGRATION→ EXPAND MARKET BY MARKET
Multi-Head deployment

One Head is only the beginning.

Medusa is designed to scale beyond a single user or a single search objective. The Head is the unit of judgement — which means one organisation can operate many independent Heads without collapsing their criteria, evidence or learning into one generic model.

INDIVIDUAL / COMPANY

Focused intelligence

One business can operate one or more Heads, each learning what that business considers worth finding and applying that judgement through repeated Hunts.

AGENT / AGENCY

Many clients. Many Heads.

An agent or agency can manage multiple client Heads from one environment. Each Head retains its own criteria, evidence, outcomes and learning while the operator gains a scalable management layer across the portfolio.

CORPORATE / ENTERPRISE

Many teams. Many territories.

A corporate deployment can support Heads across teams, business units, propositions and countries, with central governance, permissions, reporting and integrations above independently operating Heads.

EXPANSION

Scale inside the customer

Growth is not limited to acquiring another customer. A successful deployment can expand through additional Heads, users, territories and business units inside the same organisation.

THE UNIT ISN'T THE CAMPAIGN. IT'S THE HEAD.

One customer may need one Head. An agency may operate fifty. A multinational may eventually operate hundreds.

The execution partner provides the infrastructure to act at that scale. Medusa provides the judgement layer that decides what each of those Heads should pursue.

What Medusa brings

Persistent commercial judgement, evidence-led qualification, explainable opportunity selection and a learning loop built around real outcomes.

What the execution partner brings

The downstream infrastructure that turns qualified opportunities into action: CRM, outreach, workflow, sales automation, advertising, engagement or other measurable execution.

Only after that proof would we discuss a deeper technology or commercial partnership.

Built from the problem

How Medusa got here

Blueprint Code AI grew out of operating PP-AV, our own ecommerce business, and repeatedly encountering commercial problems we wanted technology to solve.

We built systems to remove friction. Those systems exposed larger problems. Those problems produced reusable ideas. Eventually, one of those ideas became Medusa Heads.

Why the partnership model works

Partners do not need to have followed the same path. The fit comes from solving adjacent layers of the same commercial process: Medusa improves the quality of what enters the execution layer, while the partner turns those inputs into action at scale.

That creates a cleaner relationship than asking either product to become the other. Medusa stays upstream; the partner keeps ownership of execution.
